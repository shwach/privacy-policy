5. 보관 기간과 삭제

기기 안의 게임 데이터는 앱의 설정에서 초기화하거나 앱을 지우면 사라집니다.

운영 서버의 세이브 복사본은 30일, 게임 안의 사건은 90일, 날마다의 접속 표시는 400일 뒤에 지웁니다. 이용자 번호에 딸린 기본 접속 정보, 의견과 답장, 순위 기록, 순위표 이름과 신고 기록은 삭제를 요청할 때까지 보관합니다. 부적절하다고 판단한 순위표 이름은 운영자가 지울 수 있습니다.

삭제를 원하면 게임 설정의 의견 보내기로 요청해 주십시오. 그 기기의 번호에 딸린 접속 정보, 게임 기록, 세이브 복사본, 의견과 답장, 순위 기록, 순위표 이름과 신고 기록을 지웁니다. 이용자 번호는 기기 안에만 있어서, 앱을 지우면 번호도 사라지고 그 뒤에는 어느 기록이 본인의 것인지 찾을 수 없습니다. 남은 기록은 위의 기간에 따라 지워집니다.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts or photos, and the app does not use the microphone or camera. Our server does not store your device model, OS version or any hardware identifier. Anything you choose to write in the feedback form is kept as written.

3. Ads and tracking

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. On iOS the advertising identifier is used for personalized ads only if you allow it in the tracking prompt. The game works the same if you decline, and you will see non-personalized ads. In regions such as Europe a consent form about ads appears first. See how Google uses information.

4. Sharing

Apart from the Apple, Google and Cloudflare services listed above, we do not sell or pass your information to anyone.

5. Retention and deletion

Game data on your device is removed when you reset the game in settings or delete the app.

On our server, save copies are deleted after 30 days, in-game events after 90 days and daily access marks after 400 days. The basic access information attached to your user number, feedback and replies, leaderboard entries, leaderboard names and reports are kept until you ask us to delete them. We may remove a leaderboard name we find inappropriate.

To request deletion, use the feedback form in the game settings. We will delete the access information, game records, save copies, feedback and replies, leaderboard entries, leaderboard names and reports attached to that device's number. The user number exists only on your device, so once you delete the app the number is gone and we can no longer tell which records were yours. Remaining records are deleted on the schedule above.

6. Age

The app imitates lottery tickets and is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children. In-game money has no cash value, and there is no way to bet or win real money.
