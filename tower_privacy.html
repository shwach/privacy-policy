개인정보처리방침

Spellbreak(이하 “본 게임”)은 계정 가입을 요구하지 않으며, 이름·이메일·전화번호·주소 같은 신원 정보를 수집하지 않습니다. 이 문서는 본 게임이 무엇을 수집하고 무엇을 수집하지 않는지 설명합니다.

Privacy Policy

Spellbreak (“the game”) does not require an account and does not collect identifying information such as your name, email address, phone number or postal address. This page explains what the game does and does not collect.

Stored only on your device

Your progress — coins, upgrade levels, research, relics, records and settings — is stored on your device only. It is never sent to a server and is removed when you delete the app.

Sent for the leaderboard

The following is sent to Google Firebase only when you use the leaderboard.

Anonymous identifierA random string issued automatically per device. It is not linked to your real name, email or phone number.
NicknameA name of up to 12 characters that you type yourself. We recommend not using your real name.
RecordDifficulty, wave reached, kill count and the time of the record.

This information is used for nothing other than displaying the leaderboard.

Advertising

The game uses rewarded ads from Google AdMob. Ads play only when you tap the button yourself; there are no forced interstitials or banners. AdMob may collect an advertising identifier and similar data in order to serve ads. On iOS you are asked once whether to allow tracking, and the game works fully if you decline.

See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles this data.

What is never collected

  • Name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • Contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera
  • Payment information (the game has no in-app purchases)

Children

The game is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Retention and deletion

Leaderboard records are kept until you ask for them to be removed. Email the address below with the nickname shown in the app and the record will be deleted. Data stored on your device can be erased by deleting the app or by using “Start over” in Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
