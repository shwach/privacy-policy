Privacy Policy

App: Orbit Idle · Last updated: 2026-07-03

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with the mobile game Orbit Idle (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not directly collect names, email addresses, or other personal information that identifies you.

  • Game data (stored on your device): Your progress, resources, robots, and settings are saved locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to us.
  • Advertising & analytics data (via third parties): The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob and its partners may collect and process information such as your device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS / Advertising ID on Android), IP address, device type and settings, and ad interaction data, in order to serve and measure ads.
  • Leaderboards (optional): If you use leaderboard features, Apple Game Center and/or Google Play Games Services may process your game-service identifier and score. This is handled by Apple/Google under their own privacy policies.

2. How Information Is Used

  • To save and restore your game progress on your device.
  • To display advertising (including rewarded and interstitial ads) and, where applicable, personalized ads.
  • To provide optional leaderboard rankings.
  • To keep the App functioning and to diagnose problems.

3. Third-Party Services

The App relies on third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — https://policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Apple Game Center — https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google Play Games Services — https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Advertising Choices

On iOS, personalized advertising uses the device advertising identifier only if you allow tracking through the App Tracking Transparency prompt; you can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, you can reset or limit ad personalization in Settings > Google > Ads.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Game data is stored locally on your device and is removed when you delete the App or clear its data. Data processed by third-party ad and game services is retained according to their respective policies.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
