Pomodoro Dungeon Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026 · Developer: Songhwa Chae · Contact: doggguggu@gmail.com

Pomodoro Dungeon ("the App") is built so that the developer does not collect personal information. This page explains what the App handles and which outside services it uses for ads and in-app purchases.

1. Information the developer collects

The developer does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number or postal address. There is no account registration or sign-in.

2. Information stored on your device

Focus timer settings, game progress (level, gold, mercenaries, expedition records and so on) and app settings such as mute are stored only on your device. They are never sent to the developer's servers and are deleted when you delete the App.

3. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to show rewarded ads, which you choose to watch. No ads are shown during a focus session. In serving ads, Google may collect, use, or share with its advertising partners device identifiers (advertising ID), coarse location, device information, ad interactions, and crash and diagnostic data, as described in Google's privacy policy.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form. On iOS, the App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether tracking is allowed. You can decline and still use the App the same way; you will see non-personalized ads. Buying the premium product grants ad rewards without ads.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment details such as card numbers are handled by Apple and Google; the developer never receives or stores them.

The App uses RevenueCat to verify, deliver and restore purchases. RevenueCat processes a random user ID created by the App and your purchase history (product, purchase time, store receipt). Your name and email address are not sent.

  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google Play Terms of Service: https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/

5. Notifications

The App may use local notifications, scheduled only on your device, to tell you when a focus session ends. Their content is never sent off the device.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 14 or 16, depending on your country), and the developer does not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe such information has been collected, please contact the address below and it will be reviewed and deleted.

7. Retention and deletion

The developer holds no user data. Progress stored on your device is erased when you delete the App. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings (iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads). To have purchase records held by RevenueCat deleted, contact the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated text and its effective date will be posted on this page.

9. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please write to doggguggu@gmail.com.
