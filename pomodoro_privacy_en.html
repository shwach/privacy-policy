15-Minute Dungeon Privacy Policy

App name: 15-Minute Dungeon (15분 던전), formerly Pomodoro Dungeon
Package name: com.pomodoro.dungeon
Developer: GGU (Songhwa Chae)
Effective date: November 2, 2026

This policy explains how 15-Minute Dungeon ("the App"), a mobile game distributed on Google Play and the App Store by the developer GGU, handles your information.

1. Information the developer collects

The developer does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or postal address. There is no registration or sign-in, and the App sends nothing to any server run by the developer. The App has no in-app purchases, so it does not handle payment information.

2. Information stored on your device

Unlocked characters, weapons and maps, gold, play records, a run in progress, and your music, sound effect, vibration, auto-pick and color settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to the developer and are erased when you delete the App. If device backup is turned on, they may be included in your Google or Apple device backup.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses rewarded ads from Google AdMob. Ads play only when you choose to watch one by tapping a button. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect the following information, and some of it may be shared with Google's advertising partners:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID
  • Approximate location estimated from your IP address
  • App interactions such as ad views and clicks
  • Crash logs and diagnostics
  • Ad-related purchase activity

This information is encrypted in transit and handled under Google's privacy policy.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Your ad choices

In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown. On iOS, the App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether tracking is allowed. If you decline, the App works the same and you will see non-personalized ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

5. App permissions

The App uses the permissions needed to show ads, such as internet, network state and advertising ID, and the vibration permission. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photo and file permissions.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child's information has been collected, please contact the address below.

7. Retention and deletion

The developer holds no user information. Progress stored on your device is erased when you delete the App. Retention and deletion of information collected by Google follow Google's privacy policy.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated text and its effective date will be posted on this page.
